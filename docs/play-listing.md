# Google Play store listing — copy

Ready to paste into Play Console → **Grow → Store presence → Main store
listing**, one locale at a time. Spanish is written first and is the source of
truth; English and Portuguese follow it rather than the other way round.

Play's limits: app name 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4000. Counts below are checked, not estimated.

Plain text only — no Markdown. Play renders asterisks and underscores literally,
so section headings are capitalised lines and bullets are `•`.

> **Before you publish this, fill in App access.** The app opens on a sign-in
> screen and needs a Padiush account with access to a project. A reviewer who
> cannot get past that screen will reject the app as non-functional, and it is
> one of the most common causes of a first-submission rejection. Play Console →
> App content → App access → provide test credentials and a one-line note that
> the account must be a member of at least one project.

### Full description

```
Padiush Companion es la aplicación de campo de la plataforma Padiush, para equipos que documentan el conocimiento tradicional sobre las plantas.

Registra entrevistas donde estés: sin cobertura, sin datos, sin depender de nada. Todo queda guardado en el teléfono, cifrado, y se sincroniza cuando vuelves a tener conexión.

NECESITAS UNA CUENTA DE PADIUSH
Esta aplicación acompaña a la plataforma web padiushbio.com. Para usarla necesitas una cuenta y que te hayan dado acceso a un proyecto. Los formularios de entrevista se diseñan en la web; aquí se responden.

PENSADA PARA EL CAMPO
• Funciona completamente sin conexión. La entrevista no espera a la señal.
• Graba audio mientras llenas el formulario, o antes de tocarlo siquiera. La grabación continúa aunque bloquees la pantalla o guardes el teléfono.
• Adjunta fotografías de la planta, del preparado o del lugar.
• Registra las coordenadas del sitio cuando el dispositivo puede darlas.
• Guarda cada respuesta según la escribes. Nada se pierde por cerrar la aplicación.
• Español, inglés y portugués.

CUIDA LO QUE TE CONTARON
• El almacén local va cifrado con SQLCipher, con una clave que se genera en el propio teléfono.
• Los archivos originales sin cifrar se borran en cuanto se incorporan.
• El contenido se elimina del teléfono cuando el servidor confirma que quedó guardado.
• Tu contraseña nunca se guarda en el dispositivo.

SINCRONIZACIÓN QUE NO PIERDE TRABAJO
Las entrevistas se envían cuando hay conexión. Si el servidor rechaza una respuesta —por ejemplo, porque la pregunta se borró en la web— la aplicación te lo dice y te deja corregirla, descartarla o reintentarla. Nada desaparece en silencio.

QUÉ NO HACE
El diseño de formularios, la vinculación con especies, los índices etnobotánicos y las exportaciones viven en la plataforma web. Aquí solo se captura.

PERMISOS
• Micrófono: para grabar la entrevista.
• Ubicación: para registrar dónde se hizo.
• Cámara y fotos: para adjuntar imágenes.
• Notificaciones: para avisar que hay una grabación en curso.

padiushbio.com
```

## English

### Short description

```
Record ethnobotanical field interviews offline, then sync them to Padiush.
```

### Full description

```
Padiush Companion is the field app for the Padiush platform, built for teams documenting traditional knowledge about plants.

Record interviews wherever you are: no coverage, no data connection, nothing to depend on. Everything is saved on the phone, encrypted, and syncs once you are back in range.

YOU NEED A PADIUSH ACCOUNT
This app accompanies the web platform at padiushbio.com. To use it you need an account and access to a project. Interview forms are designed on the web; here they are answered.

BUILT FOR FIELDWORK
• Works entirely offline. The interview does not wait for a signal.
• Record audio while you fill the form, or before you touch it at all. Recording continues when the screen locks or the phone goes in a pocket.
• Attach photographs of the plant, the preparation or the place.
• Record the coordinates of the site when the device can provide them.
• Saves every answer as you write it. Nothing is lost by closing the app.
• Spanish, English and Portuguese.

LOOKS AFTER WHAT YOU WERE TOLD
• The local store is encrypted with SQLCipher, using a key generated on the phone itself.
• Unencrypted originals are deleted as soon as they are taken in.
• Content is removed from the phone once the server confirms it was stored.
• Your password is never kept on the device.

SYNC THAT DOES NOT LOSE WORK
Interviews are sent when there is a connection. If the server refuses an answer — because the question was deleted on the web, for instance — the app says so and lets you correct it, discard it or try again. Nothing disappears quietly.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
Form design, species linking, ethnobotanical indices and exports live on the web platform. This app only captures.

PERMISSIONS
• Microphone: to record the interview.
• Location: to record where it took place.
• Camera and photos: to attach images.
• Notifications: to show that a recording is in progress.

padiushbio.com
```

## Claims to keep honest

Every line above is true of the build as it stands. Two that will date:

- **"Recording continues when the screen locks"** relies on the foreground
  service added on 2026-08-07. Verify it on a device before publishing — the
  claim is in the listing, so it is now a promise to Play as well as to users.
- **No transcription is mentioned**, matching privacy policy §8. If server-side
  transcription ships, the listing may mention it; until then it must not.
